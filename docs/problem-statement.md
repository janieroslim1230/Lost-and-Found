# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
People who lose valuable items in public spaces and those who find items and want to help return them to their rightful owners.

## What exactly is going wrong?
There is no simple, centralized way for finders and owners to connect. This creates missed opportunities to reunite people with their belongings.

## Why does it matter?
A streamlined solution would reduce stress, save time, and create trust and goodwill in a comunity by helping people recover items that matter to them.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
People who lose valuable items in public areas often struggle to reconnect with them, while those who find items lack an easy way to reach the rightful owners. The absence of a centralized system creates inefficiency and missed opportunities for recovery. By providing a platform that connects finders and owners directly, this solution can reduce stress, save time, and encourage community trust by reuniting people with their valuables.
