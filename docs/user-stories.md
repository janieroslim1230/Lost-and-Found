# User Stories
Format: As a [type of user], I want [goal], so that [reason or benefit].

1. As a person who often lost an item, I want to post details about my missing belonging, so that others can help me recover it quickly.

2. As a person who also often found an item, I want to share information about what I discovered, so that the rightful owner can identify and reclaim it.

3. As a community member, I want a secure and trustworthy platform to connect finders and owners, so that valuable items can be reunited without confusion or fraud.
